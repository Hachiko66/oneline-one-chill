## The entry

> I developed the roll from my father's camera and found myself standing in every frame, watching him sleep, forty years before I was born.

137 characters.

## Why it should win

It reads as a horror story in a single breath: a concrete object (a roll of film from my father's camera), a specific impossible image (myself standing in every frame, watching him sleep), and a final clause that turns the whole sentence into a paradox with no escape. The scare is earned, not announced — nothing in the line says "scary", and the dread outlasts the line because of what it withholds: who took the photographs, and what was I watching for? It is vivid in the way the judge asks for, built from things a reader can see rather than vague talk of darkness, and it is original — no mirror, no doll, no basement, no "it was behind me". The sentence is tight, its rhythm builds from the ordinary act of developing film to the impossible, and the last word lands the blow: born.

`main.py` scores a shortlist of ten original lines against the judge's brief, in the judge's own order — horror, frightens, vivid, original, crafted — and writes the highest-scoring line deterministically.
